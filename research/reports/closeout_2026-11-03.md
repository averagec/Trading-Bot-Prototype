## 2. Reading the result

* The live bot's loss is mostly cost (about 70%) and 10-pip stops, not a wrong-way signal. Its direction is statistically indistinguishable from random, so cost reduction can at best bring it towards zero, not above it.
* The one lever that is large and year-stable, an ATR-scaled stop, reduces drag on the null (+0.033 R) but is worth about +0.01 R on the real signals, within noise. Mechanism yes, skill no.
* Every arm that could produce a candidate was pre-registered before it was run, DEV only, with the falsifier stated; none produced one. The power of each is stated in its report. The nulls rule out large effects (roughly 0.03 to 0.10 R depending on the arm), not small ones.
* Risk arithmetic (quant review, not re-checked): at the live 0.25% risk and -0.13 R expectancy, the 10% static loss limit is breached within 12 months in 86% of simulated paths. No risk profile makes the pass probability material (0.0 to 0.7%).
